---
title: Manage your account
description: Change your name, email and password, link or remove GitHub, sign out other browsers and pick a theme from the Settings page.
---

The **Settings** page shows who you are signed in as and lets you change how you sign in, end sessions on other browsers, and choose the Studio's theme. Open it from **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. Every member and admin can use it.

## Account

The **Account** section shows your name, the email or GitHub `@login` you signed in with, and your role: **Admin** or **Member**.

## Change your name

<Steps>
  <Step>In the **Account** section, click **Edit name**.</Step>
  <Step>Type your **Name**, up to 100 characters, and click **Save name**.</Step>
</Steps>

Your team sees the new name on the **Members** page. Signing in with GitHub keeps the name you chose.

## Change your email

The email is the one you sign in with. Your password stays the same.

<Steps>
  <Step>Under **Sign-in methods**, click **Change email** in the **Password** row.</Step>
  <Step>Enter the **New email** and your **Current password**.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Change email**.</Step>
</Steps>

From then on you sign in with the new email; the old one no longer works. If another member already signs in with that email, the dialog says "Another account already signs in with this email." under **New email**.

## Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step>Under **Sign-in methods**, click **Change password** in the **Password** row.</Step>
  <Step>
    Enter your **Current password**, then a **New password** of at least 10 characters, and type it
    again in **Confirm new password**.
  </Step>
  <Step>Click **Change password**.</Step>
</Steps>

This browser stays signed in. Every other browser signed in as you is signed out, and the confirmation says how many, for example "Password changed. Signed out 2 other browsers."

If the current password is wrong, the dialog shows "Your current password is wrong." under that field. For all password rules, see [Password rules](/studio/sign-in#password-rules).

If the **Password** row says "Ask an admin for a reset link to add a password.", your account has no password yet. An admin can give you one with a [reset link](/studio/members#give-someone-a-reset-link).

## Link your GitHub account

The **GitHub** row appears when the server offers GitHub sign-in. If it says **Not linked to this account.**:

<Steps>
  <Step>Click **Link GitHub**.</Step>
  <Step>Finish signing in on GitHub.</Step>
</Steps>

GitHub sends you back to **Settings**, and the row shows your `@login`. From then on you can use **Continue with GitHub** on the sign-in screen ([Sign in with GitHub](/studio/sign-in#sign-in-with-github)).

A GitHub account can belong to only one member. If it already belongs to someone else, nothing is linked and the Studio shows, for example, "GitHub account example-dev already belongs to another member."

## Remove GitHub

When your account has both a password and GitHub, the **GitHub** row has a **Remove** button:

<Steps>
  <Step>Click **Remove** in the **GitHub** row.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Remove** again in the dialog.</Step>
</Steps>

You then sign in with your email and password only, and browsers that signed in with GitHub are signed out. You can link GitHub again later.

The **Remove** button does not appear when this browser signed in with GitHub (sign in with your password first), or when GitHub is your only way to sign in.

## Sign out other browsers

The **Signed-in browsers** section lists every browser where you are signed in, for example "Chrome on macOS". The browser you are using has a **This browser** badge. Each row says when the browser was last active and when it signed in. Hover over that line to see when the session expires.

- To sign out one other browser, click **End session** on its row.
- To sign out every browser, this one included, click **Sign out everywhere**, then **Sign out everywhere** again in the dialog. You return to the sign-in screen. Your API keys keep working.

To sign out only this browser, click **Sign out** in the account menu ([Sign out](/studio/sign-in#sign-out)).

## Choose a theme

Under **Appearance**, pick **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. **System** is the default and follows your operating system's setting. The choice is saved in this browser only, so other browsers keep their own.

You can also change the theme from **Theme** in the account menu. The sun or moon button next to the account menu switches straight between light and dark.

## When sign-in is off

If the server runs with sign-in turned off, **Account** says "Sign-in is off on this server." and the **Sign-in methods** and **Signed-in browsers** sections do not appear. **Appearance** works as usual.
